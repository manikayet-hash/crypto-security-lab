📚 Security Knowledge Center

Learn the common techniques criminals use to trick people and the basic steps that can reduce the risk.

🎣 1. Phishing

Phishing uses fake messages or websites to trick someone into revealing sensitive information.

  • Check the website address carefully.
  • Do not trust unexpected login links.
  • Never enter recovery phrases into random websites.

👛 2. Wallet Approvals

Some blockchain applications ask wallets to approve token permissions. A dangerous or unnecessary approval can create financial risk.

  • Understand what you are approving.
  • Reject unexpected requests.
  • Review and revoke permissions when appropriate.

🔑 3. Seed Phrase Theft

A wallet recovery phrase is extremely sensitive. Anyone who obtains it may be able to control the wallet.

  • Never share your recovery phrase.
  • Do not type it into websites or chat messages.
  • Store it securely offline.

🏦 4. Exchange Account Takeover

Attackers may attempt to gain access through stolen passwords, phishing, social engineering, or compromised accounts.

  • Use a unique password.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Review account security alerts.

🌐 5. Malicious Websites

A website can look almost identical to a legitimate service while using a completely different domain.

  • Inspect the domain name.
  • Be careful with shortened links.
  • Do not assume HTTPS alone proves a website is legitimate.

🧠 6. Social Engineering

Social engineering manipulates people rather than attacking technology directly.

  • Be suspicious of urgent requests.
  • Verify unusual requests through another channel.
  • Never reveal security codes to someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
🛡️ Golden Rule

Never give a stranger your password, authentication code, private key, or wallet recovery phrase.
